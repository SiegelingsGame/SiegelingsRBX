The Codex of Binding
A World Forged by Card and Shield
Before the wristguard and the Capture Card, the lands were wild. Humans fought the elements and the beasts of the frontier. Then Maestro appeared and changed everything.

The Gift of Maestro

In a time of unending war between humans and Siegelings, a mysterious figure emerged. Whether Siegeling or Eleminion, no record is certain. Maestro offered the first Capture Cards — not weapons, but instruments of covenant. A card could bind a Siegeling to a human only if the creature chose to accept.

"He gave the knowledge and then withdrew. No grave, no shrine. Only the cards, the shields, and a legend."

He taught the crafting of the Shield — a wristguard with a slot for the card. Place the card, and the creature answers. Withdraw it, and the creature returns. The bond was voluntary. Coercion broke it.

The Eleminions

Rare beings with a third eye hidden between the brows. Through it they see thoughts, translate tongues, and bridge the gap between human and Siegeling. One per elemental region. To meet one is rare; to refuse their counsel, foolish.

The Rite of Thirteen

At age thirteen, every youth receives a Shield and their first Capture Card, then sets off to find and bond their first Siegeling. This ancient ceremony marks the start of the path from Squire to Siege Knight.

Valorous vs. Unvalorous

The Valorous keep the pact: bond by consent, fight with honor, defend the realm. The Unvalorous — raiders, warlords, outcasts — stole the craft, force bonds, and lay siege to peaceful lands. The conflict has never ended.

The World
Three Zones, Infinite Adventure
The world of Sieglings is divided into three distinct zones, each with escalating challenge, richer rewards, and deeper mechanics. Progress through all three to prove your worth.
🏰
The Training Zone
Where Every Squire Begins
The Training Zone is the heart of Sieglings — a safe haven where new Squires take their first steps. Here you claim your starter Siegeling, build your fortress plot, and learn the fundamentals of capture, combat, and base management. Four elemental biomes surround a central hub: the Frozen Tundra to the north, the Verdant Forest to the east, the Highland Winds to the south, and the Ocean Shore to the west. Wild Siegelings of Common and Uncommon rarity roam freely, waiting to be bonded.

The Outer Zones
Beyond the Safety of the Training Grounds
Beyond the Training Zone lie the Outer Zones — vast, dangerous, and teeming with Rare and Epic Siegelings. The Volcanic Wastes churn with Fire creatures immune to the lava rivers that cut through the landscape. The Electric Peaks crackle with Lightning hazards that only Lightning-mounted knights can survive. The Shadow Caves twist into lightless labyrinths where Shadow Siegelings hunt in packs. To reach the Outer Zones, you must defeat four boss Siegelings and collect their sigils, then present them at the Zone Door. Once inside, you can relocate your entire base to claim a plot in the frontier.
🛡️
Move Your Base — Relocate your fortress to the Outer Zones for stronger wild spawns and higher income generation.

The Badlands
Roguelike PvPvE — Enter with Nothing, Leave with Everything
The Badlands is the endgame — a high-stakes roguelike zone where up to 8 players enter with nothing and fight to extract with the rarest creatures in the game. Every Siegeling that spawns here is Epic or Legendary rarity, Gold or Legend variant, scaled to 1.5-2x normal size with doubled stats and levels 20-50. The zone collapses over time, pushing all players toward extraction points that activate at the 7-minute mark. You have 15 seconds to channel your extraction or lose everything. The Badlands Broker manages your queue, offers daily contracts, and trades resources for advantages.
🎲
Roguelike Runs — 10-minute matches. Enter empty-handed. Capture creatures into a 10-slot Bag. Extract or lose everything.
💎
Only the Rarest — Epic and Legendary Siegelings in Gold and Legend variants with 2x stats. Massive, glowing, and deadly.
⏱️

📦
Loot & Supply Drops — Defeated creatures drop loot bags (60s despawn). Supply drops provide mid-run advantages.

The Broker — NPC who manages your queue, offers daily sacrifice contracts, and brokers high-risk trades.
🛡️
Spawn Shield — 30 seconds of invulnerability on entry. Use it to scout, position, and plan your route.
Your Path
How to Play
Every system feeds into the next. Capture creatures to earn income, upgrade your base to build battle teams, mount your Siegelings to explore, and battle to earn glory.
01

Capture & Bond

Find wild Siegelings across seven elemental biomes. Weaken them in combat with your companion, then hold your Capture Card for 2.5 seconds to bind them. Rarer creatures cost more — from 10 gold for Common up to 400 for Legendary.

02

Mount & Explore

Set a mountable Siegeling as your companion and ride it. Ground mounts sprint at boosted speed, Water mounts dive through oceans with unlimited breath, and Wind mounts soar through the sky. Fire mounts are immune to lava, Lightning mounts to electric hazards.

03

Build & Defend

Claim a plot and build up to 4 floors. Assign creatures to Income slots (earn coins), Defense slots (guard your base with turret AI), or your Battle Team (3x3 arena grid). Design your own gym on Floor 4 for visitors to challenge.

04

Battle & Conquer

Enter the King of the Hill arena, challenge players to PvP duels, raid enemy bases to steal creatures, conquer legendary dungeons, and brave the Badlands roguelike for the rarest loot in the game.

Core Systems
What Makes Sieglings Tick
Deep, interconnected systems where every decision matters — from which creature you mount to how you build your gym.
🐎

Mounting & Riding

Mount your Siegelings and traverse the world. Ground mounts boost your speed based on creature stats. Flying mounts (Wind element) let you soar at 40 studs/s. Swimming mounts (Water) let you explore the ocean floor. Each mount generates a defense shield based on its stats, absorbing damage while you ride.

🌊

Swimming & Diving

Dive into the Ocean Biome with a base breath time of 10 seconds. Without a Water mount, your health drains after time runs out. Bond a Water Siegeling and your breath extends up to 70 seconds. Explore underwater caves, battle aquatic creatures, and discover the Water Gym hidden beneath the waves.

🕊️

Flight & Gliding

Wind and Flying-type Siegelings take to the skies. Soar over Highland peaks, scout enemy bases from above, and access hidden areas only reachable by air. Wind mounts also grant a glide bonus, slowing your fall when dismounting mid-air.

🏰

Multi-Floor Fortress

Build up to four floors with 6 Income and 6 Defense slots per floor. Floor 2 unlocks the Battle Arena team system. Floor 3 maximizes your empire. Floor 4 unlocks your personal gym. Relocate your entire base to the Outer Zones for frontier bonuses.

🏋️

Build Your Own Gym

Unlock Floor 4 and design your personal gym. Set your best defenders on the battle grid. When other players visit and challenge your gym, your creatures fight automatically. Win or lose, the bounty system grows — you earn 75 coins per successful defense, and challengers earn bounty rewards for toppling you.

👑

King of the Hill Arena

Automated team battles every 60 seconds. Up to 9 creatures on a 3x3 grid. Winners grow 5% per victory (capped at 300%). Synergy bonuses stack for devastating combos. Focus bar system powers devastating special attacks.

🛡️

Defense Turret AI

Creatures on defense points act as automated turrets, firing at intruders within 40 studs every 2.5 seconds. Your first and last line against raids. Wild creature packs also attack your base periodically — keep your defenses strong.

⚔️

PvP Duels & Raids

Challenge nearby players to 1v1 creature battles. Raid online players' bases to steal up to 3 creatures per raid (50% base steal chance minus 10% per defense creature). Winners earn gold and XP; losers can spend gems to instant-revive.

🔄

Evolution & Combining

Evolve creatures along multi-stage chains (e.g. Firsky → Emberpup → Dracoil → Pyleer → Pylord). Combine three of the same variant tier — Normal → Silver → Gold → Legend — for permanent stat multipliers up to 1.6x.

🥚

Egg Hatching

Five egg tiers from Common to God Egg. Higher tiers guarantee rarer creatures. Every egg is a chance at a Legendary. Purchase with coins or Robux from the Egg Shop.

🌌

Day & Night Cycle

A full day cycles every few minutes. Night increases Silver spawn chance for commons and Gold chance for rares. Shadow and Lightning creatures remain active at night while others sleep. Atmospheric lighting shifts create a living, breathing world.

✨

Cosmetics & Style

Customize your knight with trails (fire, ice, rainbow, shadow), auras (flame, electric, divine, void, sakura), and name colors (gold, rainbow, cyan, and more). Express your identity on the battlefield and in the arena.

The Codex
Hand-Drawn Visual Codex
Every Siegeling in your Codex is a hand-drawn character with unique animations for idle, movement, attack, and special abilities. The in-game 3D Codex Viewer lets you inspect every creature from every angle.
🎨

Hand-Drawn Characters

Every one of the 90 Siegelings is hand-drawn with unique character design. Each creature has distinct silhouettes, color palettes, and proportions that reflect their element and personality — from the fiery mane of Pylord to the crystalline wings of Glaciemperor.

🎬

Custom Animations

Each creature has hand-crafted animation states: idle poses, movement gaits, attack sequences, faint animations, and special ability effects. Flying creatures hover and bank. Crawling creatures slither along the ground. Mountable creatures have distinct riding animations.

🔍

3D Codex Viewer

Open the in-game Codex to browse your creature collection in a full 3D model viewer. Rotate, zoom, and inspect every variant tier. Read lore entries, view evolution chains, check base stats, and see which biome each creature calls home.

📖

Lore & Evolution Chains

Every Codex entry includes the creature's lore, behavior type (pack, lone, gentle, aggressive, skittish), full evolution chain, stat breakdowns, and synergy bonuses. Track your collection progress across all 7 elements and 5 rarity tiers.

✨

Visual Effects

Creatures glow with elemental particle effects. Fire Siegelings emit embers, Ice creatures trail frost crystals, Shadow creatures pulse with dark energy. Legendary and Gold/Legend variants have enhanced aura effects visible from across the map.

📸

Variant Showcase

Each creature has four visual tiers: Normal, Silver (metallic sheen), Gold (luminous glow), and Legend (prismatic aura). The Codex displays all unlocked variants side by side with their stat multipliers, letting you track your combining progress.

The Valorous Path
Ranks & Terminology
Know the world before you enter it. Every knight carries a Shield, every bond begins with a Card.
Siegeling
Elemental creatures of Fire, Ice, Earth, Wind, Shadow, Lightning, and Water. Bound to humans via Capture Card and Shield.
Squire
Apprentice who begins at 13 with the Rite. Learns capture, combat, and base management. Every player starts here.
Siege Knight
Full rank earned after proving yourself. Noble defenders of the realm who bond Siegelings and duel in the arena.
Siegelord
Elite knights of legendary status. Lead realms, sit on councils, and defend frontiers. The best of the best.
Sire
The single highest-ranked knight. Head of the Council of Siegelords. "Everybody is Ser. Only one is Sire."
Shield
Wristguard device with a slot for the Siegeling card. Bears a sigil declaring house, order, or personal allegiance.
Capture Card
Enchanted card created by Maestro. Binds a Siegeling to a human. The bond requires the creature's consent — coercion breaks it.
Maestro
Mysterious figure who gave humans the first Capture Cards. Believed to be Eleminion or progenitor of their kind. Vanished — may still watch over the bonded.
Eleminion
Rare creatures with a third eye. Understand both humans and Siegelings. One per elemental region. Translators between species.
The Badlands Broker
Mysterious NPC at the entrance to the Badlands. Manages the queue, offers daily sacrifice contracts, and trades resources for advantages in the roguelike zone.
Sigil
Token dropped by boss Siegelings. Collect four sigils to unlock progression to deeper territories through the Zone Door.
Extraction
The act of escaping the Badlands with your captured creatures. Channel for 15 seconds at an extraction point. Fail, and you lose everything from that run.
