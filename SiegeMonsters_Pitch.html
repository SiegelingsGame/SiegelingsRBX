Core Systems
What Makes Siege Monsters Tick

Every system feeds into the next — capture creatures to earn income, earn income to upgrade your base, upgrade your base to build battle teams, and battle to earn glory and steal from rivals.

🌍

World Creature Spawning

Wild creatures spawn across elemental biomes at SpawnPoints, DungeonPoints, and BossPoints. Pack creatures travel in groups. Bosses are one-per-biome legendaries with 5-minute respawn timers. Up to 150 creatures in the world (plus night bonus). Day/night cycle affects spawns and variants.

Live
🎯

Creature Capture

Faint wild creatures first, then capture within 30 studs. Each capture costs coins by rarity (10 Common to 400 Legendary). Captured creatures go to your inventory (max 50). Starter choice: Cacty, Breezee, Pylme, Sundile, or Ceeponee.

Live
🏰

Multi-Floor Base System

Each player owns a plot with up to 3 floors. Floor 1 is free with 6 income + 6 defense slots. Floor 2 unlocks at Player Level 2 (500 coins) and adds the Battle Arena team (9-slot grid, max 5 creatures). Floor 3 at Level 5 (5,000 coins) for full slots and Combiner/Recycler.

Live
💰

Passive Income Economy

Creatures placed on Income Points generate coins every 10 seconds based on their baseIncome stat. Common creatures earn 1 coin/tick, Legendaries earn 50. Income also awards 2 Player XP per tick. Stack your base for maximum earnings.

Live
🛡️

Defense Turret System

Creatures on Defense Points act as automated turrets, attacking world creatures and enemy companions within 40 studs every 2.5 seconds. Defense creatures protect your base during raids and AI invasions. 6 slots per floor.

Live
🐾

Companion System

Set a favorite creature and it follows you as a companion with its own HP, attack AI, level-scaled stats, and faint/respawn cycle (30s cooldown). Toggle attack/passive mode, manually target enemies. Supports custom 3D mesh models.

Live
⚔️

Player Combat

Ranged (25 studs, 0.8s cooldown, 4 damage) and Melee (10 studs, 8 stud AOE, 10 damage). Rebirth level adds +5% world damage per level. All damage routes through CreatureAI. Creatures faint at 0 HP and become capturable.

Live
👑

King of the Hill Arena

Automated PvP arena battles every 60 seconds. Teams of up to 5 creatures (9-slot grid) auto-battle with speed-based turn order. Winners grow 5% per win (up to 300%). Synergy bonuses from matching elements and classes stack for massive advantages.

Live
🏴‍☠️

Player Raiding

Raid online players' bases to steal creatures. 30% base steal chance. Max 1 steal per raid. 120s cooldown; 60s protection for victims. Steal mechanic: faint base creatures, carry them home within 30 studs of your plot to deliver.

Live
🤖

Creature AI Behaviors

6 distinct behavior types: Aggressive (hunts players), Pack (group coordination with call range), Gentle (peaceful unless provoked), Lone (territorial), Skittish (flees on approach), and Stationary (base creatures). Pack members alert each other when attacked.

Live
🔒

Laser Door Shield

Activate a protective dome shield over your base (50 stud radius). Lasts 50 seconds, blocks monsters and raiders. Up to 10 friends can be whitelisted to pass through. Intruders take 20 damage and are pushed out.

Live
🥚

Egg Hatching Shop

Five egg tiers: Common (150 coins / 25 R$ — Common/Uncommon/Rare), Rare (800 / 75 R$ — Uncommon/Rare), Mythic (3,500 / 199 R$ — Rare/Epic), Legendary (12,000 / 499 R$ — Rare/Epic/Legendary), God (50,000 / 999 R$ — Epic/Legendary). Eggs hatch over time when placed on base.

Live
⚡

Buff Shop

Temporary power-ups: Energy Shield (60s, 150 coins), Super Jump (45s, 100 coins), Super Speed (60s, 120 coins), Invulnerability (15s, 5 gems), Invisibility (30s, 3 gems), Swap Places (instant, 200 coins).

Live
✨

Cosmetic Shop

Trails (Fire, Ice, Rainbow, Shadow), Auras (Flame, Electric, Divine), and Name Colors (Gold, Red, Rainbow). Coins and Gems currencies. Equip/unequip at any time.

Live
📊

Dual Leveling System

Creature Levels: Base form max 10, evolved 25, final form 50. Rank-based stat growth, +8% base per level. XP from captures, battles, defense kills.
Player Levels: Max 50, gates floors and content. XP from captures (15), arena wins (50), income (2), raids (30), dungeon (20), boss (100).

Live
🏠

Base Exterior & Color Shop

Full themes: Haunted House, Retro Arcade (500 coins). Color themes for backwall, walls, and stairs (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange). Base Color Shop tints walls, stairs, points, Combiner, Recycler (300 coins per color).

Live
🔄

Combiner & Recycler

Combiner (Floor 3): Combine 3 same-creature same-variant into next tier (Normal→Silver→Gold→Legend).
Recycler: Trade 3+ duplicate same creature for 1 egg one rarity tier higher. Place egg on base to hatch over time.

Live
✋

Base Interaction

Walk up to base creatures: pick up, move, swap slots, or sell (within 12 studs). Place/swap on income and defense points from 18 studs. Sell price: captureCost × level. Proximity prompts on Combiner/Recycler at base.

Live
🌙

Day / Night Cycle

Configurable day/night (e.g. 180s per full cycle). At night: higher max world creatures, chance for Silver/Gold spawn variants, and world creatures with evolution lines can temporarily evolve.

Live
⚡

Focus Bar & Special Attacks

Arena/PvP: focus builds from attacks (25 per hit). At 100 focus, unleash element specials — Fire Burn (8% max HP/round), Earth damage reduction debuff, Wind focus drain (50). 2.5s special animation.

Live
🔁

Pilot Rebirth

Prestige system: spend gold + creatures (by rarity) to rebirth. Resets progress but grants permanent bonuses: +2 passive gold/tick per level, +5% world damage per level, +15 max HP per level. Max rebirth level 10.

Live
🏠

Home Recall

Channel 5 seconds (stay in radius) to teleport to your base. Interrupted by taking damage. Heaven-beam style visual. Optional quick return from the world.

Live
🐲

Custom 3D Creature Models

Creatures use custom mesh models from ReplicatedStorage. Supported for world spawns, companion, and base. Flying, crawling, and placement-offset options per creature.

Live

Future Vision
Development Roadmap

Current live systems form the core loop. The next phases expand depth, progression, and monetization.

Phase 1 — Foundation (Complete)

Core Game Loop

All fundamental systems live and working:

  • 90 creatures across 6 elements, 5 classes, 5 rarities; evolution chains & variant combine (Normal→Silver→Gold→Legend)
  • World spawning (150 max + night bonus), SpawnPoints, DungeonPoints, BossPoints; day/night cycle with Silver/Gold spawn variants
  • Capture (faint-first, 30 studs), inventory (50), starter choice (Cacty, Breezee, Pylme, Sundile, Ceeponee)
  • Multi-floor base: Floor 1 free (6 income + 6 defense), Floor 2 at Lv.2/500 coins (battle team), Floor 3 at Lv.5/5,000 (Combiner/Recycler)
  • Base Exterior Shop (Haunted House, Retro Arcade, color themes), Base Color Shop; base interaction (pick up, move, swap, sell)
  • Combiner (3 same → next variant), Recycler (3 duplicates → egg one tier higher)
  • Companion system, steal-to-base mechanic; Focus bar & element specials in arena/PvP
  • King of the Hill arena, PvP 1v1, player raiding (30% steal, 1/raid), laser door, AI raids
  • Egg shop (5 tiers: Common→God), buff shop, cosmetic shop
  • Creature levels (base 10 / evolved 25 / final 50), player level 50; Pilot Rebirth (10 levels, permanent bonuses)
  • Home Recall (5s channel), custom 3D creature models (flying, crawling, placement offsets)
Phase 2 — Arena Expansion

King of the Hill Rewards Overhaul

Deeper rewards and competitive structure:

  • Win Streak Rewards — Escalating coin/gem rewards based on consecutive wins
  • Team Bounty System — Team value calculated from total creature worth; winners claim percentage of defeated team's bounty
  • Leaderboard Seasons — Track cumulative bounty earned over time periods
  • Arena Exclusive Drops — Rare creatures only available through arena wins at high streaks
Phase 3 — Base Expansion

Base Design, Improvements & Upgrades

Transform bases from functional to personal:

  • Tower Defense Structures — Buildable turret towers, walls, and traps placed on defense points
  • 2nd & 3rd Floor Visual Upgrades — Cosmetic themes and structural enhancements per floor
  • Exterior Placements — Decorative and functional items outside the main base area
  • Upgrade Paths — Invest coins/gems to level up base structures for increased defense stats
Phase 4 — Raid Overhaul

Advanced Raiding System

Full raid gameplay loop:

  • Steal Defender Monsters — Successfully defeating base defenses lets you steal defense creatures
  • Full Base Raid — Break through all defenses to access income monsters as well
  • Raid Replays — View replays of raids on your base while you were away
  • Raid Leagues — Competitive raiding tiers with exclusive rewards
Phase 5 — World Expansion

New Portal Zones & Map Design

Expand the world with new biomes and creatures:

  • Portal Zones — New areas accessed through portals, each with unique elements and monsters
  • PvE Spawn Areas — Dedicated zones for challenging PvE encounters with scaling difficulty
  • Biome-Specific Events — Timed events unique to each portal zone
  • New Elements — Expand beyond the current 6 elements with portal-exclusive types
  • Map Design Overhaul — Richer environments, exploration rewards, hidden areas
Phase 6 — Evolution & Fusion

Monster Evolution & Combining System

Deep progression for creature collectors:

  • Evolution Chains — Creatures evolve into stronger forms at specific levels (e.g., Chillpuff → Frostag → Glaciemperor)
  • Monster Combining — 3 like creatures combine into a Silver variant, 3 Silvers → Gold, 3 Golds → Legend
  • Fusion Lab (Top Floor) — Dedicated base area for fusing creatures into higher-tier versions with boosted stats
  • Corrupted Monsters — Any element creature can become "Corrupted" — dark variant with altered stats and unique abilities
Phase 7 — Monetization & Retention

Shops, Passes & Daily Engagement

Sustainable monetization and player retention:

  • Player Buff Shop (Expanded) — Shields, High Jumps, Super Speed, Invulnerability, Invisibility, Swap Places (already in GameConfig)
  • Cosmetic Shop (Expanded) — More trails, auras, name effects, creature skins
  • Dailies, Weeklies, Monthlies — Rotating challenge system with escalating rewards
  • Battle Pass — Seasonal progression track with free and premium reward tiers
Under the Hood
Technical Architecture

36+ Lua scripts with clean module separation, authoritative server-side HP tracking, and DataStore persistence.

🧠

Server Architecture

Single entry point (MainServer.lua) initializes all modules in strict dependency order. All game logic runs server-side. Clients only send input and receive state updates via RemoteEvents.

💾

Data Persistence

PlayerDataManager handles all DataStore operations. Tracks coins, gems, inventory (with unique UIDs), slot assignments, battle teams, player level/XP, owned floors, friends, buffs, cosmetics, base exterior/color, rebirth level, and stats.

🔗

Module Interactions

CreatureAI is the authoritative HP system — all damage routes through it. BasePlacementSystem handles visual state. FavoriteCreatureSystem manages companion AI. BattleTeamSystem computes synergies.

🎮

Client Systems

HUDButtonBar provides 8-button menu bar with keyboard shortcuts. PlayerCombatClient handles input. PlayerProfileClient shows stats. All UI modular and event-driven.
