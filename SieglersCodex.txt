I. QUICK REFERENCE — CONTROLS & GAMEPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CONTROLS
--------
  [W][A][S][D]  — Move
  [E]           — Target nearest siegling (for capture or combat)
  [F]           — Ranged / Melee attack (when targeting)
  [Q]           — Inventory (creatures, base layout, battle team)
  [R]           — Egg Shop (buy and hatch eggs)
  [G]           — Buff Shop (power-ups)
  [C]           — Cosmetic Shop (trails, auras)
  [V]           — Friends (base access list)
  [X]           — Leaderboards
  [P]           — Player Profile
  [Z]           — Rebirth (reset for permanent bonuses)
  [H]           — Home Recall (channel 5 seconds to return to base)
  [B]           — Battle tab (in Inventory — assign creatures to arena/PvP)
  [Y]           — Toggle favorite companion (in Inventory)

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
  1. CAPTURE — Find sieglings in the world. Press [E] to target. Attack with
     [F] (and your companion) until the creature faints. Click the fainted
     creature to capture (costs gold; rarer = more cost).

  2. COMPANION — Set one creature as your Favorite in Inventory. That siegling
     follows you and fights by your side in the wilds.

  3. BASE — You have a plot with slots. Assign creatures to:
     • Income  — Earn coins over time
     • Defense — Guard your base from raids
     • Battle  — Fight in arena and PvP (unlock Floor 2)

  4. ARENA — Team battles for fame and rewards. Unlock via base upgrade.

  5. PVP — Challenge nearby players to 1v1 duels.

  6. RAIDS — Attack other bases to steal creatures. Defend with your defense team.

  7. COMBINE — Three of the same variant tier (Normal/Silver/Gold) combine into
     the next tier. Use the Combiner at your base.

================================================================================

You have taken the Rite of Thirteen. You hold your shield, you hold your first
Capture Card. In the flame-lands of the frontier, we say: Through flame, we hold.

This world turns on sieglings—the creatures bound to us by card and shield. As a

wilds.

III. CHAPTER ONE: The Shield and the Card
        Lady Elara Frostholm, House Frostholm (Ice)
================================================================================

Your SHIELD is more than armor. It is a wristguard with a slot for your siegling
card. Place a card into the slot, and the creature bound to it is summoned to
your side. Withdraw the card, and it returns to the vault.

Every shield bears a SIGIL. Ours declares house and allegiance. Hedge knights
carry personal sigils they may pass to their Squires. Orders share sigils
between members. Great houses wear one sigil for all—a mark of loyalty.

Guard your shield. Guard your cards. They are the proof of your bond.

================================================================================
IV. CHAPTER TWO: Capturing Your First siegling
        Lord Marcus Cinderthorn, House Cinderthorn (Earth)
================================================================================

Capture is the heart of the path. You weaken a wild siegling in combat—your
strikes and your COMPANION's attacks—until it faints. Then you use a Capture
Card. Success costs gold; the rarer the creature, the higher the cost.

TIP: Set one creature as your FAVORITE. That siegling becomes your companion and
follows you into the wilds, fighting by your side. Choose well—your companion
will be your first line of defense and offense.

Do not force the bond. A siegling that refuses the card cannot be bound. Patience
and respect matter more than strength.

================================================================================
V. CHAPTER THREE: Your Base and Your Roles
        Lord Kael Zephyran, House Zephyran (Air)
================================================================================

Every Squire is granted a PLOT—your home in this land. On it you assign your
sieglings to three roles:

  • INCOME  — Your sieglings work the land. Coins flow over time.
  • DEFENSE — Your sieglings stand guard. Raiders who attack your base must face
              them. Build a strong defense.
  • BATTLE TEAM — Your sieglings fight for you in the arena and in duels. Unlock
                  Floor 2 to access the battle grid. You may field up to five
                  sieglings in a 3×3 formation.

Choose based on each creature's strengths. Fire for offense, Ice for control,
Earth for endurance, Air for speed. Build a balanced team. Swift as the storm
means knowing when to strike and when to hold.

================================================================================
VI. CHAPTER FOUR: The Arena and Honor Duels
        Lord Theron Emberward, House Emberward (Fire)
================================================================================

The arena is where the Valorous prove themselves. Knights and Siegelords battle
daily for fame, territory, and the people's amusement. You will enter that arena.
You will lose and you will win. Both teach.

Arena battles pit teams against each other. PvP duels let you challenge nearby
knights. Your battle team fights; you command. Learn the focus bar, special
attacks, and synergies. Fire burns, Earth slows, Wind drains focus, Ice freezes.

Fight with honor. Win or lose, you represent your house and the Valorous way.

================================================================================
VII. CHAPTER FIVE: Dungeons, Raids, and the Unvalorous
        Lady Elara Frostholm, House Frostholm (Ice)
================================================================================

Beyond the safe paths lie DUNGEONS—stronger sieglings, higher risk, greater
reward. Rarer creatures lurk there. Face them when you are ready.

Beware RAIDS. Other knights may assault your base to steal your sieglings.
Defend with your defense team. And remember: not all who wield sieglings are
Valorous. Some lay siege to these lands—raiders, warlords, outcasts. They take
by force and fight without honor. Our duty is to stand against them.

Cold steel, warm heart.

================================================================================
VIII. CHAPTER SIX: Evolving and Combining
        Lord Marcus Cinderthorn, House Cinderthorn (Earth)
================================================================================

sieglings can grow stronger. EVOLUTION advances a creature to a higher form.
COMBINING takes three of the same variant tier—Normal, Silver, Gold—and merges
them into the next. Three Normals become one Silver. Three Silvers become one
Gold. Three Golds become one Legend.

House Cinderthorn holds the forges and the combiner. Use it. A stronger team
means a stronger defense.

Forge and defend.

================================================================================
                    THE CODEX OF BINDING
        A History of the sieglings and the Gift of Maestro
================================================================================

Transcribed from the Archives of the Council of Houses.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I. THE AGE BEFORE CARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the earliest chronicles, before the wristguard and the Capture Card, the lands
were wild. Humans fought the elements and the beasts of the frontier. Among
those beasts were the sieglings—creatures of flame, frost, stone, and wind—who
lived as rivals and predators.

Yet whispers survived of beings unlike the rest. Beings that spoke in the mind.
They wore a third eye, hidden between the brows, and through it they saw the
thoughts of others. They understood human speech. They could translate the
tongues of the sieglings. The chronicles name them Eleminions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
II. THE ELEMINIONS AND THE THIRD EYE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each elemental region holds one Eleminion. Fire, Ice, Earth, Air. They live in
secluded places. They are neither fully siegling nor fully human. Their third
eye pierces illusion, reads intention, and opens a bridge between minds.

Through this gift they understand both human and siegling. They translate
meaning, emotion, and nuance. A knight may speak to his siegling only because
an Eleminion first taught how such speech might work. The Four Houses guard
their whereabouts. To meet one is rare; to refuse their counsel, foolish.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
III. MAESTRO AND THE FIRST CARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The deepest mystery is Maestro. Whether siegling or Eleminion, no text is
certain. Some claim he was the progenitor of the Eleminions—the first of their
kind. Others say he was an Eleminion who ascended, or a siegling who transcended.

What is recorded: Maestro appeared in a time of crisis. Humans and sieglings
fought without end. Then Maestro emerged—from where, none could say—and offered
the first Capture Cards. Not weapons, but instruments of covenant. A card could
bind a siegling to a human only if the creature chose to accept. The bond was
voluntary. Coercion broke it.

He taught the crafting of the shield. The wristguard with its slot for the card.
Place the card, and the creature answers. Withdraw it, and the creature returns.
He gave the knowledge and then withdrew. He vanished. No grave, no shrine. Only
the cards, the shields, and a legend: that Maestro might still watch over those
who honor the bond.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IV. THE VALOROUS AND THE UNVALOROUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Not all who hold cards are worthy. Raiders and warlords stole the craft. They
learn to force bonds, to break the compact Maestro intended. They lay siege to
peaceful lands. The chronicles call them the Unvalorous. The Valorous keep the
pact: bond by consent, fight with honor, defend the realm. The conflict has
never ended.

                              GLOSSARY
================================================================================

siegling (pl. sieglings)
  Elemental creatures of Fire, Ice, Earth, Air (and faraway: Shadow, Lightning).
  Bound to humans via Capture Card and shield. Have their own language.

SIEGE KNIGHT
  A knight who bonds sieglings and duels in the arena. Noble defenders of the
  realm. Full rank after Squire.

SQUIRE
  Apprentice who serves under a Knight. Learns capture and combat. Earns
  Knighthood after proving themselves. Begins at 13 with the Rite.

SIEGELORD
  Elite knights; legendary status. Lead realms, sit on councils, defend
  frontiers. Best in the arenas.

SIRE
  The single highest-ranked knight. Head of the Council. Everybody is Ser;
  only one is Sire.

SER
  Honorific for all knights. "Ser" before a name.

SHIELD
  Wristguard device with a slot for the siegling card. Placing the card
  summons the creature. Bears a sigil (house, order, or hedge knight).

CAPTURE CARD
  Enchanted card that binds a siegling. Given by Maestro to humankind. Bond
  requires the creature's consent.

RITE OF THIRTEEN
  Ceremony at age 13. Youth receives shield and first Capture Card, then sets
  off to find and bond their first siegling.

ELEMINION
  Rare creatures with a third eye. Understand humans and sieglings. Act as
  translators. One per elemental land. Can read minds and interpret language.

MAESTRO
  Mysterious figure who gave humans the first Capture Cards. Believed to be
  Eleminion or progenitor of their kind. Vanished; may still watch over the
  bonded.

VALOROUS
  Honorable Siege Knights who bond by consent, fight with honor, and defend
  the realm.

UNVALOROUS
  Raiders, warlords, outcasts who force bonds and lay siege to peaceful lands.
  Fight without honor.

SIGIL
  Mark on the shield declaring identity. Hedge knights: personal, passed to
  Squire. Orders: shared. Great Houses: same for all members.

FOUR HOUSES
  Emberward (Fire), Frostholm (Ice), Cinderthorn (Earth), Zephyran (Air).
  Govern this land. Each associated with an Eleminion.

COUNCIL OF SIEGELORDS
  Ruling body of the finest knights. The Sire sits at its head.
