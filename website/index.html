The Codex of Binding
A World Forged by Card and Shield
Before the wristguard and the Capture Card, the lands were wild. Humans fought the elements and the beasts of the frontier. Then Maestro appeared and changed everything.

The Gift of Maestro

In a time of unending war between humans and Siegelings, a mysterious figure emerged. Whether Siegeling or Eleminion, no record is certain. Maestro offered the first Capture Cards — not weapons, but instruments of covenant. A card could bind a Siegeling to a human only if the creature chose to accept.

"He gave the knowledge and then withdrew. No grave, no shrine. Only the cards, the shields, and a legend."

He taught the crafting of the Shield — a wristguard with a slot for the card. Place the card, and the creature answers. Withdraw it, and the creature returns. The bond was voluntary. Coercion broke it.

The Eleminions

Rare beings with a third eye hidden between the brows. Through it they see thoughts, translate tongues, and bridge the gap between human and Siegeling. One per elemental region. To meet one is rare; to refuse their counsel, foolish.

The Rite of Thirteen

At age thirteen, every youth receives a Shield and their first Capture Card, then sets off to find and bond their first Siegeling. This ancient ceremony marks the start of the path from Squire to Siege Knight.

Valorous vs. Unvalorous

The Valorous keep the pact: bond by consent, fight with honor, defend the realm. The Unvalorous — raiders, warlords, outcasts — stole the craft, force bonds, and lay siege to peaceful lands. The conflict has never ended.

Your Path
How to Play
Every system feeds into the next. Capture creatures to earn income, upgrade your base to build battle teams, and battle to earn glory and steal from rivals.
01

Capture

Find wild Siegelings across six elemental biomes. Weaken them in combat and use your Capture Card to bind them. Rarer creatures cost more — from 10 gold for Common up to 400 for Legendary.

02

Companion

Set one creature as your Favorite. Your companion follows you everywhere, fights by your side with scaled stats, and has its own HP and respawn cycle. Choose your battle partner wisely.

03

Build Your Base

Assign creatures to Income slots (earn coins), Defense slots (guard your base), or Battle Team slots (arena fighting). Expand from 1 floor to 3 for maximum power and slots.

04

Battle & Raid

Enter the arena for team battles, challenge players to PvP duels, raid enemy bases to steal creatures, and conquer dungeons for rare loot. Rise from Squire to Sire.

Core Systems
What Makes Sieglings Tick
Deep, interconnected systems create emergent gameplay where every decision matters.
🏰

Multi-Floor Fortress

Build a three-story base with 6 income + 6 defense slots per floor. Floor 2 unlocks the Battle Arena and team system. Floor 3 maximizes your empire.

👑

King of the Hill Arena

Automated team battles every 60 seconds. Up to 9 creatures on a 3x3 grid. Winners grow 5% per victory (capped at 300%). Synergy bonuses stack for devastating combos.

🛡️

Defense Turret AI

Creatures on defense points act as automated turrets, firing at intruders within 40 studs every 2.5 seconds. Your first and last line against raids.

⚔️

PvP Duels

Challenge nearby players to 1v1 battles. Winners earn gold and glory, losers can spend coins or gems to instant-revive their fainted team.

🥚

Egg Hatching

Five egg tiers from Common to God Egg. Higher tiers guarantee rarer creatures. Hatch times scale with creature level. Every egg is a chance at a Legendary.

🔄

Evolution & Combining

Evolve creatures to higher forms. Combine three of the same variant tier — Normal to Silver to Gold to Legend — for permanent power upgrades.

🌌

Day & Night Cycle

A full day cycles every 3 minutes. Night brings 35% Silver spawn chance for common creatures, 25% Gold chance for rares, and wild evolution of base forms.

🛡️

Laser Door Shield

Activate a protective energy dome over your base. Lasts 50 seconds, blocks all intruders. Whitelist up to 10 friends. Trespassers take damage and get pushed out.

✨

Rebirth System

Reset for permanent bonuses: extra gold per tick, world damage multipliers, and max health boosts. Ten rebirth levels for long-term progression.

The Valorous Path
Ranks & Terminology
Know the world before you enter it. Every knight carries a Shield, every bond begins with a Card.
Siegeling
Elemental creatures of Fire, Ice, Earth, Wind, Shadow, and Lightning. Bound to humans via Capture Card and Shield.
Squire
Apprentice who begins at 13 with the Rite. Learns capture, combat, and base management. Every player starts here.
Siege Knight
Full rank earned after proving yourself. Noble defenders of the realm who bond Siegelings and duel in the arena.
Siegelord
Elite knights of legendary status. Lead realms, sit on councils, and defend frontiers. The best of the best.
Sire
The single highest-ranked knight. Head of the Council of Siegelords. "Everybody is Ser. Only one is Sire."
Shield
Wristguard device with a slot for the Siegeling card. Bears a sigil declaring house, order, or personal allegiance.
Capture Card
Enchanted card created by Maestro. Binds a Siegeling to a human. The bond requires the creature's consent — coercion breaks it.
Maestro
Mysterious figure who gave humans the first Capture Cards. Believed to be Eleminion or progenitor of their kind. Vanished — may still watch over the bonded.
Eleminion
Rare creatures with a third eye. Understand both humans and Siegelings. One per elemental region. Translators between species.
